# SCENORA Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 12 November 2026

SCENORA is a social movie-review application created as an academic software
development project. This policy explains what information the application
uses and how it is handled.

## Information we collect

SCENORA may collect the information that a user provides when creating or
using an account, including:

- name, username, and email address;
- basic Google profile information when the user chooses Google Sign-In;
- optional profile information;
- movie reviews, ratings, likes, comments, follows, watchlist entries, and
  Shoutbox messages; and
- authentication and session information required to keep the account signed
  in and protect access to it.

SCENORA does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or other
private Google services. Google Sign-In is used only to obtain the user's basic
identity, email address, and profile information for authentication.

## How we use information

Information is used to:

- create and authenticate SCENORA accounts;
- display profiles and community contributions;
- provide review, rating, social, watchlist, and Shoutbox features;
- maintain security and prevent unauthorized account access; and
- operate, troubleshoot, and improve the application.

## Data storage and third-party services

SCENORA stores application data in Supabase PostgreSQL and operates its backend
through Render. Movie searches and metadata are provided through The Movie
Database (TMDB). Google provides authentication when Google Sign-In is chosen.
These providers process limited data as necessary to deliver their respective
services and are governed by their own privacy policies.

## Sharing and sale of data

SCENORA does not sell personal information. Content intentionally posted to
community features, such as reviews, ratings, comments, profile information,
and Shoutbox messages, may be visible to other SCENORA users.

## Data retention and deletion

Information is retained while it is needed to operate the account and the
application. A user may request correction or deletion of their account and
associated personal information by contacting the project team. Some limited
information may be retained when required for security, legal compliance, or
backup recovery.

## Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect account and application
data. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, and users
should protect their own account credentials.

## Children's privacy

SCENORA is not intentionally directed to children under 13, and the project
team does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as SCENORA changes. Updates will be published in
this repository with a revised effective date.
